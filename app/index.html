How to run a test 3 × 1 minute on the Dummy

Set up

  1. Equip the build you're testing. Training Ground uses whatever is on your build screen: characters, gear, runes, mystling and artifacts.
  2. Open Training Ground and set the enemy unit type to Dummy.
  3. Leave main weapon attack on. Don't use the grant level-up skill or grant Valkyrie skill buttons.
  4. You have to pick one skill at the start. Refresh until you're offered one that doesn't affect damage (Max HP increase is ideal), then pick it.

Run it three times

  1. Tap Reset so the timer and damage counter start at zero.
  2. Attack the dummy. Move however you like; check the movement tips for this build.
  3. When the timer hits 1:00, pause and screenshot the timer and damage counter (top right).
  4. Write down the damage and the exact time shown. A second or two over is fine; the page adjusts for it.
  5. Unpause, tap Reset, and repeat until you have three runs. Don't change anything between runs. If Reset asks for a skill again, pick the same non-damage skill.

Quick start

Our guild dashboard shows the roster, your builds, and every Expedition plan and result in one place.

Get set up (5 minutes)

  1. Open the dashboard link and sign in with a free Claude account.
  2. On the Roster, tap your name, then This is me to link your account.
  3. Check your Inventory and fix any stars marked ★?.
  4. On Builds, make your Expedition preset match the one you use in game. Officers plan lanes from it.

The tabs

  • Inventory and Builds: your items and saved builds, plus build suggestions. The whole guild can see your inventory, but only you and officers can change it. Your builds stay private, except the Expedition preset.
  • Roster: every member's power, gear, artifacts and Expedition damage, whether they've linked their account, and a link to their inventory.
  • Expedition: each round's lineup, the plan, results, the week's results, and your damage history (under Roster).
  • Training Ground: test a build on the Training Dummy and post your score.
  • What's next: what's being worked on and planned, plus a place to send ideas and report bugs.
  • Version number (top right): tap it for the Change log (what's new in the dashboard) and the Activity log (who changed shared data).

Good to know

  • Picks are based mostly on gear set and your average damage per second over your last 6 rounds using your current build preset. A strong round moves you up.
  • Copy as image buttons make pictures you can paste into Discord.
  • Questions? Ping an officer or Daby in Discord.
Heads up: nothing changes until you press a save button, and every shared change is logged. Officers can edit shared data, so read Editing permissions below before making changes.

⚠ Editing permissions, and how not to change things by accident

Who can change what:

  • Everyone: your own Inventory, builds and Training Ground results, plus your own Expedition preset. Your inventory and Expedition preset are visible to the whole guild; your other builds stay private.
  • Officers: everything shared. That means any roster profile, any player's inventory, gear pieces on the Roster, lineups for unplayed rounds, opponents, messages to the officers and notes. Officers are also editors of the dashboard page itself.

Nothing changes until you press a save button. Tapping a gear piece on the Roster, opening Edit lineup or opening a message box only opens an editor. Press Cancel, or just leave without saving, and nothing changes.

Tips to avoid accidental edits:

  • Officers: be careful tapping gear pieces on the Roster while scrolling on a phone. If an editor pops open, tap Cancel.
  • Check the player's name at the top of an editor before you press Save.
  • Notes and messages to the officers can't be edited or deleted after posting. Read them over first. To correct one, post a new message or a note that fixes it.
  • Played rounds are locked. Round results come from the Details screenshots, so if something looks wrong, tell Daby instead of working around it.
  • Don't edit or republish the dashboard page itself from claude.ai, even though officers have editor access. Ask Daby, so every version stays logged and can be rolled back.

Made a mistake? Every shared change is recorded in the activity log (tap the version number at the top, then Activity log), with who made it and when. Tell an officer or Daby and it can be put back.

Inside the Expedition tab: pick a week at the top, then one of five views.

  • Rounds: each round's lanes for us and the opponent, the Message to the officers explaining the plan, notes and reasoning, and Round details with lane results, fight lengths and damage. Played rounds are read-only.
  • Week results: the week so far after each round (record, standings, each round's key notes, top DPS), then the final results and what we learned once Round 3 is in.
  • Roster: everyone's gear set (Mixed means pieces from more than one set: hover it on a computer, or open the player's page, to see which), character with resonance under it, Avg DPS, best round, and a graph of their last 6 rounds (hover a dot for the week and round). Tiles show their roster scans, lane history, each player's damage and seconds survived, and habits for that week.
  • Findings: what we've learned so far, how Expedition works, and the assumptions behind the numbers.

Guild names link to the Opponents view, and player names link to that player's history (with a ← Back button to return).

Common tasks

Update your Expedition preset. Change it on the Builds tab and save, or use Edit my preset in your Roster profile. The two stay in sync, and whichever you changed last wins. Do this whenever you change your in-game preset, since officers plan lanes from it.

Check how you did in Expedition. Go to Expedition, then Roster, and tap your name. You'll see your damage, seconds survived and DPS for every round, plus your average over your last 6 rounds using your current build preset. ← Back at the top returns to the roster.

Read the plan for a round. Go to Expedition, then Rounds, and pick the round. The Message to the officers at the top explains the lineup. Tap Show the full message if it's cut off.

Share something on Discord. Look for the Copy as image buttons on the lineup, round results, officer message, player history, the Expedition roster table and the main Roster. The picture goes to your clipboard, ready to paste. If your browser blocks that, a preview opens with a Download button instead. On the officer message, Copy text copies it with its Discord formatting.

See who changed something. Tap the version number at the top right, then Activity log. Filter by person or type of change, or search for a player or field. Each entry shows what changed, before → after. Members' entries say "(member)", and results Claude adds from screenshots say "by Claude". Export (CSV) downloads everything.

Send an idea or report a bug. Go to What's next, then Ideas & bug reports. Pick Idea or Bug, give it a short title and details, and press Send. Everyone can see it, and officers reply right on it. You can edit or withdraw it until an officer replies.

Test a build. On Training Ground, pick a saved build, run the Training Dummy 3 times for 1 minute each, and enter your results. Post them to the leaderboard or keep them private.

Get a build idea. On Builds, open Want a suggestion? and tap Suggest a build. It only uses what's in your Inventory, and it tells you what's missing for a better option.

For officers

Officers (editors of the dashboard) can change shared data. Every change is recorded in the activity log (tap the version number at the top, then Activity log).

  • Roster: edit any profile, tap a gear piece to change its set and quality, add members, mark members inactive or left.
  • Inventories: on the Roster, tap a player's Inventory cell to open it, edit, and press Save to (their name). They get your changes the next time they open the dashboard, and the edit is logged.
  • Preset screenshots: at the bottom of the Roster tab, import Expedition preset screenshots. Each screenshot needs the player's name typed in. The dashboard never guesses whose preset it is.
  • Lineups: edit the lineup of a round that hasn't been played yet, slot by slot, or copy the previous round. Played rounds are locked.
  • Message to the officers: post a new message for a round. Pasting a Discord message keeps its formatting. Older messages move into the notes.
  • Notes and reasoning: add decisions or logic worth keeping, like why a player moved lanes. Notes can be added but not edited.
  • What's next: add, edit or remove items on Upcoming features, and reply to ideas and bug reports with a status (the reply is public). Add to Upcoming features turns an idea or bug report into a planned item, credited to whoever sent it; Remove from Upcoming features takes it back off.
  • Suggested lineups: ask for a suggested lineup for an upcoming round and apply it with one tap.
  • Opponents: add each week's opponents (up to 3 per week) and update their details. Use Edit this opponent on the guild's tile.

Round results come from the Details screenshots shared after each round. Please keep sending all three Details screens, ours and theirs. Daby's weekly chat collects them and Claude adds them to the dashboard in batches; those changes show in the Activity log as "by Claude".

Key terms
TermWhat it means
LaneOne of the three Expedition routes: Left, Middle, Right. Up to 8 deployed players each. Win 2 of 3 to win the round.
Deployed playersThe 24 players placed in lanes for a round.
SubsMembers not deployed that round. It's about matching gear to the mode, not a judgment on anyone.
AnchorThe player in a lane with the most artifact stars. Their artifacts set the lane's HP bonus for everyone in it.
Lane HPThe anchor's HP bonus: 18% per Mythic artifact star plus 1.8% per Legendary star.
ATK bonusYour personal artifact attack bonus: 0.25% per Mythic star plus 0.025% per Legendary star.
DPSDamage per second in Expedition: your damage divided by the seconds you survived.
Avg DPSYour average DPS over your last 6 counted rounds. Rounds on a different gear set aren't counted.
Gear setDragoon, Oracle or Griffin, based on your weapon. On the dashboard the colors are red for Dragoon, blue for Oracle and gold for Griffin. Mixed means pieces from more than one set.
NotableA flag on the Expedition roster for big changes only: a gear switch, a huge jump or drop, a new personal best, or a strong trend. Most players have none, and that's normal.
Expedition presetThe separate loadout you use in Expedition. It's usually close to your main loadout, but not always.
FAQ

My info on the Roster is wrong. How do I fix it? Update your Expedition preset on the Builds tab or with Edit my preset on your profile. For anything else, like rank or power, ping an officer.

I saved my preset but the Roster didn't change. Check that you linked the right profile with This is me. If it's still off, refresh the page, then ping an officer.

Who can see my inventory? Everyone in the guild, so officers can plan with real data. Only you and officers can change it, and your builds stay private (except your Expedition preset).

Why wasn't I deployed this round? Lanes are picked mainly by gear set and your DPS average from recent rounds, then artifacts. Players on Mythic gear or with empty preset slots usually sit out. Strong rounds and a complete preset move you up.

Why don't I have a DPS average? You need at least one counted Expedition round. Rounds on a different gear set or with missing screenshots don't count.

Can I see other guilds' characters? No. We only record characters from our own preset screens, since profile screens aren't reliable.

Do I need the dashboard to know the plan? No. Officers post the plan in Discord, and anyone can share images from the dashboard there.

Something's broken or confusing. Ping an officer or Daby in Discord. Tap the version number at the top first and check the Change log, since a recent update may explain it.
